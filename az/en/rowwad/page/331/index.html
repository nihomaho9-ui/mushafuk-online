Rowwad Translation Center — 331

21:102 They will not hear its slightest hissing, and they will abide forever in whatever their souls desire.

21:103 They will not be grieved by the Greatest Terror[1], and the angels will receive them [saying], “This is your Day that you were promised”.

21:104 On the Day when We roll up the heavens like a scroll of records. Just as We originated the first creation, so We will bring it back. That is Our binding promise, which We will surely do.

21:105 We have written in the Psalms[1] after the Preserved Record[2], that the land will be inherited by My righteous slaves.

21:106 Indeed, there is a message in this [Qur’an] for devoted worshipers.

21:107 We have not sent you [O Prophet] except as a mercy to the worlds.

21:108 Say, “It is only revealed to me that your God is only One God; will you then submit to Him?

21:109 But if they turn away, say, “I have proclaimed the message to you all alike[1], and I do not know whether what you are warned of[2] is near or far.

21:110 Indeed, He knows what is said openly and knows what you conceal.

21:111 I do not know; perhaps this [delay][1] is a test for you[2] and an enjoyment for a while.”

21:112 [The Prophet] said, “My Lord, judge [between us] in truth. Our Lord is the Most Compassionate Whose help is to be sought against what you describe[1].”
