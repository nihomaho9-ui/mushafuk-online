Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali, Muhammad Muhsin Khan — 458

38:84 84. (Allâh) said: "The Truth is - and the Truth I say -

38:85 85. That I will fill Hell with you [Iblîs (Satan)] and those of them (mankind) that follow you, together."

38:86 86. Say (O Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم): "No wage do I ask of you for this (the Qur’ân), nor am I one of the Mutakallifûn (those who pretend and fabricate things which do not exist).

38:87 87. "It (this Qur’ân) is only a Reminder for all the ‘Âlamîn (mankind and jinn).

38:88 88. "And you shall certainly know the truth of it after a while".

39:1 1. The revelation of this Book (the Qur’ân) is from Allâh, the All-Mighty, the All-Wise.

39:2 2. Verily We have sent down the Book to you (O Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم) in truth: So worship Allâh (Alone) by doing religious deeds sincerely for Allâh’s sake only.

39:3 3. Surely the religion (i.e. the worship and the obedience) is for Allâh only. And those who take Auliyâ’ (protectors, helpers, lords, gods) besides Him (say): "We worship them only that they may bring us near to Allâh." Verily Allâh will judge between them concerning that wherein they differ. Truly, Allâh guides not him who is a liar, and a disbeliever.

39:4 4. Had Allâh willed to take a son (or offspring), He could have chosen whom He willed out of those whom He created. But glory be to Him! (He is above such things). He is Allâh, the One, the Irresistible[1].

39:5 5. He has created the heavens and the earth with truth. He makes the night to go in the day and makes the day to go in the night. And He has subjected the sun and the moon. Each running (on a fixed course) for an appointed term. Verily, He is the All-Mighty, the Oft-Forgiving.
