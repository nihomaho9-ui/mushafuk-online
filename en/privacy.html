Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026.

Mushafuk.online is for reading the Madinah mushaf page by page.

The site is published by Nibras Code.

What we do not collect

There is no account.

We do not take your name, email, phone, location, contacts, or device id.

There are no ads, analytics, or trackers.

Data is not sold or shared.

What stays on the device

Language, text size, and night mode stay on this device.

The tafsir, translation, i‘rab, and gharib book you pick stay on this device.

Bookmarks and notes stay on this device only.

They are not sent to a server.

Search

Search looks only at the Quran text, translations, and tafsirs that are on this site.

If the question is in Azerbaijani, Turkish, English, or Russian, the answer comes from that language’s translation.

The question is not sent to an outside model.

Internet and recitation

Internet is used to open a page, a tafsir, a translation, i‘rab, rare words, and the audio.

Audio opens through the EveryAyah service.

The connection is HTTPS.

This connection is not used to build a profile of the reader.

Children

The site does not knowingly collect data from children.

The content is the Quran, and there are no ads.
