Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali, Muhammad Muhsin Khan — 224

11:20 20. By no means will they escape (from Allâh’s Torment) on earth, nor have they protectors besides Allâh! Their torment will be doubled! They could not bear to hear (the preachers of the truth) and they used not to see (the truth because of their severe aversion, in spite of the fact that they had the sense of hearing and sight).

11:21 21. They are those who have lost their ownselves, and their invented false deities will vanish from them.

11:22 22. Certainly, they are those who will be the greatest losers in the Hereafter.

11:23 23. Verily, those who believe (in the Oneness of Allâh - Islâmic Monotheism) and do righteous good deeds, and humble themselves (in repentance and obedience) before their Lord, they will be dwellers of Paradise to dwell therein forever.

11:24 24. The likeness of the two parties is as the blind and the deaf and the seer and the hearer. Are they equal when compared? Will you not then take heed?

11:25 25. And indeed We sent Nûh (Noah) to his people (and he said): "I have come to you as a plain warner."

11:26 26. "That you worship none but Allâh; surely, I fear for you the torment of a painful Day."

11:27 27. The chiefs who disbelieved among his people said: "We see you but a man like ourselves, nor do we see any follow you but the meanest among us and they (too) followed you without thinking. And we do not see in you any merit above us, in fact we think you are liars."

11:28 28. He said: "O my people! Tell me, if I have a clear proof from my Lord, and a Mercy (Prophethood) has come to me from Him, but that (Mercy) has been obscured from your sight. Shall we compel you to accept it (Islâmic Monotheism) when you have a strong hatred for it?
