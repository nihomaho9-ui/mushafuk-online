Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali, Muhammad Muhsin Khan — 269

16:15 15. And He has affixed into the earth mountains standing firm, lest it should shake with you; and rivers and roads, that you may guide yourselves.

16:16 16. And landmarks (signposts during the day) and by the stars (during the night), they (mankind) guide themselves.

16:17 17. Is then He, Who creates as one who creates not? Will you not then remember?

16:18 18. And if you would count the favours of Allâh, never could you be able to count them. Truly! Allâh is Oft-Forgiving, Most Merciful.

16:19 19. And Allâh knows what you conceal and what you reveal.

16:20 20. Those whom they (Al-Mushrikûn[10]) invoke besides Allâh have not created anything, but are themselves created.

16:21 21. (They are) dead, not alive; and they know not when they will be raised up.

16:22 22. Your Ilâh (God) is One Ilâh (God - Allâh, none has the right to be worshipped but He). But for those who believe not in the Hereafter, their hearts deny (the faith in the Oneness of Allâh), and they are proud.

16:23 23. Certainly, Allâh knows what they conceal and what they reveal. Truly, He likes not the proud.

16:24 24. And when it is said to them: "What is it that your Lord has sent down (unto Muhammad صلى الله عليه و سلم)?" They say: "Tales of the men of old!"

16:25 25. They may bear their own burdens in full on the Day of Resurrection, and also of the burdens of those whom they misled without knowledge. Evil indeed is that which they shall bear!

16:26 26. Those before them indeed plotted, but Allâh struck at the foundation of their building, and then the roof fell down upon them, from above them, and the torment overtook them from directions they did not perceive.
