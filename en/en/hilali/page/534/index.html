Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali, Muhammad Muhsin Khan — 534

55:70 70. Therein (Gardens) will be Khairâtun-Hisân [fair (wives) good and beautiful];

55:71 71. Then which of the Blessings of your Lord will you both (jinn and men) deny?

55:72 72. Hûr[4] (beautiful, fair females) guarded in pavilions;

55:73 73. Then which of the Blessings of your Lord will you both (jinn and men) deny?

55:74 74. With whom no man or jinni has had tamth[5] before them.

55:75 75. Then which of the Blessings of your Lord will you both (jinn and men) deny?

55:76 76. Reclining on green cushions and rich beautiful mattresses.

55:77 77. Then which of the Blessings of your Lord will you both (jinn and men) deny?

55:78 78. Blessed be the Name of your Lord (Allâh), the Owner of Majesty and Honour.

56:1 1. When the Event (i.e. the Day of Resurrection) befalls.

56:2 2. And there can be no denial of its befalling.

56:3 3. Bringing low (some - those who will enter Hell) Exalting (others - those who will enter Paradise). [Tafsir Ibn Kathir]

56:4 4. When the earth will be shaken with a terrible shake.

56:5 5. And the mountains will be powdered to dust,

56:6 6. So that they will become floating dust particles.

56:7 7. And you (all) will be in three groups.

56:8 8. So those on the Right Hand (i.e. those who will be given their Records in their right hands) - how (fortunate) will be those on the Right Hand! (As a respect for them, because they will enter Paradise).

56:9 9. And those on the Left Hand (i.e. those who will be given their Record in their left hands) - how (unfortunate) will be those on the Left Hand! (As a disgrace for them, because they will enter Hell).

56:10 10. And those foremost [(in Islâmic Faith of Monotheism and in performing righteous deeds) in the life of this world on the very first call for to embrace Islâm] will be foremost (in Paradise).

56:11 11. These will be the nearest (to Allâh).

56:12 12. In the Gardens of Delight (Paradise).

56:13 13. A multitude of those (foremost) will be from the first generations (who embraced Islâm).

56:14 14. And a few of those (foremost) will be from the later generations.

56:15 15. (They will be) on thrones woven with gold and precious stones.

56:16 16. Reclining thereon, face to face.
