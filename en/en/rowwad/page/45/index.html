Rowwad Translation Center — 45

2:265 And the likeness of those who spend their wealth, seeking the pleasure of Allah and fortifying their own souls, is that of a garden on a fertile hill[1]; if it is hit by a downpour, it yields double its harvest. And if it is not hit by a downpour, then a drizzle is sufficient. And Allah is All-Seeing of what you do.

2:266 Would any of you wish to have an orchard of date palms and grapevines, under which rivers flow, full of all kinds of fruits; but he grew old and had feeble young children, then it is hit by a fiery whirlwind, burning it all[1]? This is how Allah makes the verses clear to you so that you may reflect.

2:267 O you who believe, spend in charity from the good things you have earned and of what We have produced for you from the earth[1]. Do not choose inferior things for charity, which you yourselves would not take except by overlooking. And know that Allah is Self-Sufficient, Praiseworthy.

2:268 Satan threatens you with poverty[1] and orders you to commit shameful acts[2], but Allah promises you forgiveness and bounty from Him. And Allah is All-Encompassing, All-Knowing.

2:269 He gives wisdom to whom He wills[1], and whoever is given wisdom is surely given abundant good, but none will take heed except people of understanding.
