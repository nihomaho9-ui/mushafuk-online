Rowwad Translation Center — 396

28:85 Indeed, the One Who has ordained for you the Qur’an will bring you back home[1]. Say, “My Lord knows best who has come with guidance and who is clearly misguided.”

28:86 You were not expecting the Book to be revealed to you, but it was a mercy from your Lord. So never be a supporter to the disbelievers[1].

28:87 Do not let them turn you away from Allah’s verses after they have been sent down to you. Rather, call people to your Lord, and never be among those who associate partners with Allah.

28:88 Do not invoke with Allah another god; none has the right to be worshiped except Him. Everything will perish except His Face[1]. His is the Judgment and to Him you will all be brought back.

29:1 Alif Lām Mīm[1].

29:2 Do people think once they say, “We believe,” that they will be left without being put to the test?

29:3 We certainly tested those who came before them. For Allah will surely make known those who are truthful and those who are liars.

29:4 Do the evildoers think that they can escape Us? How evil is their judgment!

29:5 Whoever hopes to meet Allah, the time appointed by Allah is sure to come, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing.

29:6 Whoever strives, he only strives for his own good. Indeed, Allah is in no need of the worlds [i.e., mankind, jinn, and all that exists].
