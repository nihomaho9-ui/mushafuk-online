Rowwad Translation Center — 216

10:62 Indeed, the allies of Allah will have no fear, nor will they grieve.

10:63 Those who believe and fear Allah.

10:64 For them are glad tidings in the life of this world[1] and in the Hereafter; there is no change in Allah’s Words. That is the supreme triumph.

10:65 Do not let their words grieve you. Indeed, all power belongs to Allah. He is the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing.

10:66 Indeed, to Allah belongs all those who are in the heavens and all those who are on earth. Those who invoke [so-called] partners besides Allah follow nothing but conjecture and they do nothing but lie.

10:67 It is He Who made the night for you to rest therein and the day bright. Indeed, there are signs in this for people who listen.

10:68 They say, “Allah has taken a son.” Glory be to Him! He is the Self-Sufficient. To Him belongs all that is in the heavens and all that is on earth. You have no proof for this. Do you[1] say about Allah something that you do not know?

10:69 Say, “Those who fabricate lies against Allah will never succeed.”

10:70 A short enjoyment in this life, then to Us is their return, then We will make them taste the severe punishment because of their disbelief.
