Rowwad Translation Center — 357

24:54 Say, “Obey Allah and obey the Messenger. But if you turn away, he is only responsible for his duty[1] and you are responsible for your duty[2]. If you obey him, you will be guided. The duty of the Messenger is only to convey the message clearly.”

24:55 Allah has promised those among you who believe and do righteous deeds that He will make them successors in the land, as He made those who came before them, and He will surely establish for them their religion which He has chosen for them, and He will surely change their state of fear into security; that they worship Me, not associating anything with Me. But whoever disbelieves[1] after that, it is they who are the rebellious.

24:56 Establish prayer, give zakah and obey the Messenger, so that you may be shown mercy.

24:57 Do not think that the disbelievers can escape in the land. Their abode will be the Fire. What a terrible destination!

24:58 O you who believe, let your slaves and those among you who have not reached puberty ask for your permission [to enter] at three times: before dawn prayer, when you take off your outer garments at noon, and after the night prayer; three times of privacy[1] for you. Beyond that there is no blame on you nor on them to move about freely, attending to one another. This is how Allah makes the verses clear to you, for Allah is All-Knowing, All-Wise.
