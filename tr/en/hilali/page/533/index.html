Muhammad Taqi-ud-Din al-Hilali, Muhammad Muhsin Khan — 533

55:42 42. Then which of the Blessings of your Lord will you both (jinn and men) deny?

55:43 43. This is the Hell which the Mujrimûn (polytheists, criminals, sinners) denied.

55:44 44. They will go between it (Hell) and the fierce boiling water!

55:45 45. Then which of the Blessings of your Lord will you both (jinn and men) deny?

55:46 46. But for him who[1] fears the standing before his Lord, there will be two Gardens (i.e. in Paradise).[2]

55:47 47. Then which of the Blessings of your Lord will you both (jinn and men) deny?

55:48 48. With spreading branches.

55:49 49. Then which of the Blessings of your Lord will you both (jinn and men) deny?

55:50 50. In them (both) will be two springs flowing (free).

55:51 51. Then which of the Blessings of your Lord will you both (jinn and men) deny?

55:52 52. In them (both) will be every kind of fruit in pairs.

55:53 53. Then which of the Blessings of your Lord will you both (jinn and men) deny?

55:54 54. Reclining upon the couches lined with silk brocade, and the fruits of the two Gardens will be near at hand.

55:55 55. Then which of the Blessings of your Lord will you both (jinn and men) deny?

55:56 56. Wherein both will be Qâsirât-ut-Tarf [chaste females (wives) restraining their glances, desiring none except their husbands], with whom no man or jinni has had tamth[3] before them.

55:57 57. Then which of the Blessings of your Lord will you both (jinn and men) deny?

55:58 58. (In beauty) they are like rubies and coral.

55:59 59. Then which of the Blessings of your Lord will you both (jinn and men) deny?

55:60 60. Is there any reward for good other than good?

55:61 61. Then which of the Blessings of your Lord will you both (jinn and men) deny?

55:62 62. And besides these two, there are two other Gardens (i.e. in Paradise).

55:63 63. Then which of the Blessings of your Lord will you both (jinn and men) deny?

55:64 64. Dark green (in colour).

55:65 65. Then which of the Blessings of your Lord will you both (jinn and men) deny?

55:66 66. In them (both) will be two springs gushing forth.

55:67 67. Then which of the Blessings of your Lord will you both (jinn and men) deny?

55:68 68. In them (both) will be fruits, and date-palms and pomegranates.

55:69 69. Then which of the Blessings of your Lord will you both (jinn and men) deny?
