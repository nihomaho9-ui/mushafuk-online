Aaya 43

Tafsir Al-Mukhtasar

وَأَقِيمُوا۟ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَءَاتُوا۟ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَٱرْكَعُوا۟ مَعَ ٱلرَّٰكِعِينَ ﴿٤٣﴾

Tottiree juulde timminon dosɗe mayre e baɗɗiiɗi e sunnaaji mayre, njaltinee asakal jawle mon ɗe Alla resndi on, njankinanoɗee Alla hawtude e yankinaniiɓe Mo iwde e leñol Muhammadu (jkm).
Aaya ɓennuɗoAaya garoowo
