Aaya 1

Tafsir Al-Mukhtasar

بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ ﴿١﴾

Ko innde Alla puɗɗorat-mi janngude Kur'aana o, mi wallinoo Mo mi barkinora e jantaade innde Makko. Basmala o na renndini inɗe Alla tati he inɗe Makko moƴƴe ɗe, ɗeen ngoni: 1- "Alla" woni: deweteeɗo e goonga, ko ndee ɓuri heeraade e inɗe Alla Toowɗo o, hay gooto innirtaake nde so wonaa Kanko Mbo senaande woodani. 2- "Arrahmaanu" woni: jooma yurmeende yaajnde. Kanko tigi woni Arrahmaanu. 3- "Arrahiimu": jooma yurmeende jokkunde yottiinde. O yurmoo mbo O welaa e tagoore Makko, goonɗinɓe e jeyaaɓe Makko na tawee heen.
Aaya garoowo
